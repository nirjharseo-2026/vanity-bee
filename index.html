Thinking about a colour change?

The wine red above was done in our chair. Deep reds and burgundy shades look rich on black hair, but they need the right base to show up.

When you book, tell us if your hair has been coloured, henna-dyed or rebonded before. We'll look at it in person, talk through the shade and how long it will take, and only then start mixing.
